# Course plan: Mountain 9 (prototype), task C0

> Proposed by the course designer, **approved by Alexis (owner) on 2026-11-18**. Built from `docs/HOLE_GUIDELINES.md` and `docs/GENRE_NOTES.md`. One hole per PR (C1–C9). Details (exact shapes, hazards, green slopes) are settled in each hole's PR; Alexis plays and comments on each one.

## Signature idea: the creek
One mountain stream winds through the whole course. On some holes it's the **shortcut** (the hero line), on others it's the **thing to stay away from**, and on two holes it widens into a **pond** and a **lake**. Hills are on every hole regardless (GENRE_NOTES: "hills, not fields").

The alternative offered was "the slope" (every hole plays its elevation differently); the owner chose the creek.

## Checks against the guidelines
- **Par 36:** 2 par 3s (3, 6), 5 par 4s (1, 2, 5, 7, 8), 2 par 5s (4, 9). No par 3 at hole 1 or 9.
- **Lengths** inside the guide ranges (par 3 110–190 m, par 4 260–400 m, par 5 420–500 m). Par 5s reachable in two only on the hero line (Driver 215 m + 3 Wood 195 m, plus downhill roll).
- **Pacing:** 1–2 warm-up (diff. 1), 3–5 introduce (2), 6–7 test (3), 8–9 climax (3–4). No two big jumps in a row; hole 8 is a high-variance hole that's an easy par when played safe, before the finale.
- **Directions:** N, E, S, W rotate so the wind changes from hole to hole.
- **Shapes:** straight (1), dogleg right (2, 5), dogleg left (4), a real corner to cut (5), split fairway (7), drivable (8), cape-style around a lake (9).
- **Greens:** flat (1, 4), one slope (2), tilted (3), two-tier (5, 7), raised (6, 9), a ridge that's hard to read (8).
- **Water on hero lines or the bad side**, never in the middle of the safe line. The only carry on the safe route is hole 4's short pitch over the creek to the green (short for everyone).
- **No blind shots:** high tees (3, 9) show the whole hole; the creek and the pond sit in visible dips with a rough or rock lip.
- **Engine features only** (no D2–D7): water, sand, rough, rock, trees, boulders, terrain. One tee and one cup per hole.

## Hole notes

**1. mtn-01 (par 4, 340 m, diff. 1).** A welcoming opener in the spirit of screenshot 8: a straight, wide, mostly flat fairway sloping gently down, set in rolling ground. One shallow bunker on the left of the landing zone. The creek is visible far right; drives down the right side get the best angle into a big flat green.

**2. mtn-02 (par 4, 300 m, diff. 1).** A short dogleg right, gently uphill. A stand of pines on a patch of rough fills the inside of the corner; the brave drive goes over them, and clipping the leaves costs distance, not a stroke. The green has one clear back-to-front slope.

**3. mtn-03 (par 3, 150 m, diff. 2).** The first real look at the creek: from a high tee the whole hole is in view below. The creek bends around the front-left of a green that tilts toward it. The safe shot plays to the right half; the pin is tucked left behind the bend.

**4. mtn-04 (par 5, 450 m, diff. 2).** A gentle dogleg left. The creek crosses in front of the green. Most players lay up and pitch over; a strong drive opens the chance to carry the creek and reach in two. Shallow fairway bunkers on the outside of the turn.

**5. mtn-05 (par 4, 360 m, diff. 2).** Screenshot 1's lesson: the creek widens into a pond inside a dogleg right, with bunkers on the outside of the landing zone. Carrying the pond leaves a wedge; going around leaves a mid-iron. Two-tier green.

**6. mtn-06 (par 3, 160 m, diff. 3).** Uphill to a raised green (screenshot 3). The creek runs left and behind the green; a grassy bank on the right feeds balls onto the green for the safe play. One deep greenside bunker cut into the bank.
*Built (C6): 160 m instead of 175 m, because at 175 m uphill the tee shot needed a 3 Wood; now it's a 5 Iron (PM OK on #50). The feeder bank is mown as fairway, because rough stopped the ball instead of feeding it.*

**7. mtn-07 (par 4, 390 m, diff. 3).** The creek splits the fairway lengthwise. Left: a wide lower fairway and a longer approach over a bunker. Right: a narrow upper shelf with a clear angle into a two-tier green. The creek has a rock lip along the shelf.
*Built (C7): about 394 m. The creek ends in a small pond between the routes, short of the green (a creek that splits a fairway has to end somewhere without crossing a route). A fairway bunker catches long drives on the left, and the shelf runs downhill, because at first the shelf saved only 6 m.*

**8. mtn-08 (par 4, 280 m, diff. 3).** Short and downhill: a big hitter can drive the green, threading between a small pond on the left and deep bunkers on the right. The safe play is an iron to a flat plateau and a wedge. The green has a ridge that's hard to read; the cup sits on a gentle part.
*Built (C8): plays about 272 m (inside the PM's ≥ 260 m), with a 12 m drop in two steps. The creek feeds the pond down the left side.*

**9. mtn-09 (par 5, 490 m, diff. 4).** The finale (screenshots 4 and 7): a big drop from a high tee with a view, the hole wrapping around the lake where the creek ends. Biting off the lake on the second shot reaches in two; the safe route plays up the left in three. A raised green with a slope and tree line behind it as a backdrop.
*Built (C9): about 489 m; the tee is about 20 m up (not 18), the lake was moved toward the green and an apron added between them, because at first the lake was easy to carry but the green was out of reach in two.*
