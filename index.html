Hole 1 · Par 3 · 166 yards

  1. Aim with ◀ ▶ (arrow keys or the buttons). The yellow ring shows where a full-power shot lands.
  2. Pick a club with ▲ ▼ or the ‹ › buttons. The best club is chosen for you.
  3. Swing in three clicks (mouse, tap, or Space):
    1st click starts the meter,
    2nd click sets power (the ⚑ mark is roughly the pin),
    3rd click sets accuracy: stop on the green line to hit it straight. Too early hooks left, too late slices right.
  4. Watch the wind arrow (top right). It pushes the ball in the air.

Sink it in as few strokes as you can. Water costs a penalty stroke.
