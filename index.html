      How to Play

      1. Aim with the ◀ ▶ buttons or arrow keys. The yellow ring shows where the ball lands.
      2. Pick a club with ‹ › or ▲ ▼. The best club is chosen for you.
      3. Swing in three clicks (tap, click or Space): the 1st starts the meter, the 2nd sets power (the bar is in quarters, like the ticks on the aim line; the ⚑ shows a putt's power), the 3rd sets accuracy: stop on the green line for a straight shot. Early hooks left, late slices right.
      4. Watch the wind (top right) and the lie: rough and sand cap your power (the gray part of the meter).
      5. Water and out of bounds cost a penalty stroke. Sink it in as few strokes as you can.
